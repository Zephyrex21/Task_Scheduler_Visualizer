First Come First Serve (FCFS)

FCFS executes processes in order of arrival. Simple but can cause long waiting times.

Characteristics:
  • Simple to implement
  • No starvation
  • Non-preemptive
Best Use Cases:
  • Batch systems
  • Equal priority processes
